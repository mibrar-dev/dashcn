- **auth-01 link rows**: `sign_in_screen.dart`, `sign_up_screen.dart` and

  "No account yet?Create one" ran together. Added
  `Gap(appTheme.spacing.xs)` between label and link in all three (sign-up's
  row reads "Have an account?"; same fix).
- **settings-01 home vertical centring**: `home_screen.dart` wrapped the
  column in `Center`, so short content floated mid-screen. Now
  `Align(topCenter)` — top-aligned like a real settings list.
- **settings-01 toggle rows**: `_ToggleRow` (home) and `Settings01ToggleRow`
  (detail screens) were bare `Row`s with no padding. Both now use
  `Padding(EdgeInsets.symmetric(horizontal: spacing.lg, vertical: spacing.sm))`
  — theme-dependent list-row density matching the nav rows.
- **`initialScreen` parameter** (brief's screen-map API): all five flows take
  `initialScreen` (default `'/'`), wired to `Navigator(initialRoute:)`, with
  `test/registry/flows/flow_initial_screen_test.dart` (light+dark: every

  back to their lists instead of throwing on the arguments cast — small
  defensive guards in `checkout-01/flow.dart` and `chat-01/flow.dart`).
- P9-3a screenshots regenerated (`flows_screenshots_test`, 34 pass).
  Before/after: exactly the touched screens changed (14/34 PNGs) —
  `auth-signin/signup/otp × light/dark` (gap shift, e.g. signin_light
