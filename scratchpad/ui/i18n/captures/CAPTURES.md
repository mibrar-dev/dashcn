Notes:

- Arabic glyphs render through a test-only `fontFamilyFallback` to the macOS
  system Arabic face (`GeezaPro`/`SFArabic.ttf` loaded in the capture test);
  Geist ships no Arabic. The fallback lives in the test, not the kit.
- Digits stay LTR everywhere (pagination wraps page numbers in
  `Directionality.ltr` / `DashcnLtr`); the ar captures show `2026` and day
  numbers left-to-right inside the RTL flow.
- Chevrons mirror: date-picker header, breadcrumb separator, pagination and
  feature-carousel arrows swap sides in RTL (asserted in
  `i18n_rtl_test.dart`, visible in the ar captures).
- No overflow at 375 px in ar, de or en (asserted per capture via
  `tester.takeException()`, and in `i18n_rtl_test.dart`).
